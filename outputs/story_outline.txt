3. **Loss and Consequences**

**IV. Act Three: Ethical Crossroads**
1. **Reckoning**

2. **Resolution of the Dilemma**

3. **Aftermath**
   - Analyze the broader societal, professional, and personal impacts of Eli’s decisions.

**V. Conclusion**
